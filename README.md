# Online Retail Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes an online retail dataset to find useful business insights about revenue, customers, products, and sales trends.  
The analysis was done using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

## Objectives
- Clean and prepare retail sales data.
- Analyze total revenue, total products sold, and total orders.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Find top-selling products and top customers.
- Generate business insights and recommendations.

## Dataset Features
The dataset contains the following columns:
- InvoiceNo
- StockCode
- Description
- Quantity
- InvoiceDate
- UnitPrice
- CustomerID
- Country

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Data Cleaning
The following cleaning steps were performed:
- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing values.
- Removed rows with missing CustomerID.
- Removed negative Quantity and UnitPrice values.

## Analysis Performed
- Total revenue calculation
- Total products sold
- Total number of transactions
- Average product price
- Monthly revenue trends
- Monthly quantity trends
- Top-selling products
- Top customers
- Country-wise business insights

## Key Insights
- Revenue shows strong growth during the holiday season.
- November and December are the busiest months.
- February is one of the slowest months.
- A few affordable products contribute to a large share of sales.
- Some customers generate very high revenue compared to others.

## Business Recommendations
- Stock more products before the holiday season.
- Plan promotions during slower months.
- Keep top-selling low-cost items available in inventory.
- Give special offers to high-value customers.
- Explore growth opportunities in more countries.
